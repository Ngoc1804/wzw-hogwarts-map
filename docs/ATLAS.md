## Phần cần nghiên cứu bổ sung

- Detailed Hogwarts floors outside approved templates
- Room-to-room graph not present in most templates
- Cross-map identity resolution
- Year and campaign visibility overlays
- Canon citations for each spatial relation
- Custom locations supplied by campaign
